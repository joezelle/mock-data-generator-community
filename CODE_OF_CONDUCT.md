# Code of Conduct

This is a place to make a developer tool better. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity, and everyone is expected to help keep it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, especially with people who are new to the tools involved.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Keep criticism about the work, and make it specific enough to act on.
- Respect people's time: search before posting, and include what's needed to help you.
- Respect privacy: don't post other people's code, data or personal details without permission.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising, and repeatedly posting off-topic material.
- Posting malware, exploits against users, or links to pirated software.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove posts, lock threads, and block people from the repository when this code is broken. Usually a reminder comes first; serious or repeated problems may lead straight to a block.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (the **…** menu), or contact the maintainer at buddiekodex@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in this repository's issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere else someone represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
